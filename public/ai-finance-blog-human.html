■  Key Takeaways
  • AI tools deliver real, documented gains — users report savings rate improvements of 2–3 percentage points and 57% less time on manual tracking.
  • The risk is trust transfer, not the technology — 52% of users who acted on GenAI financial advice made a poor decision, with errors concentrated in tax, investment sequencing, and debt strategy.
  • The correct posture: AI as co-pilot, not autopilot — use it for pattern analysis and routine decisions; keep human judgment in the loop for anything high-stakes.
