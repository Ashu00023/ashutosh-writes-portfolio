YouTube Description

Someone earning ₹50,000 a month in 2015 is taking home ₹1,20,558 today. Looks like a win — until you run it through inflation, and it's worth ₹70,578 in real terms. Ten years of discipline, and the system handed them a pay cut.

This video breaks down why the standard advice — spend less, save more, invest early — isn't wrong, just incomplete. It walks through the actual mechanical difference between labour income and capital income, why India's tax code treats the two so differently, and the one decision (not a bigger salary) that actually changes the trajectory.

Includes real math on SIP compounding, why doubling your salary doesn't get you out of the trap, and a step-by-step on where to start with as little as ₹500 a month.

This channel is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser. This video is educational content only, not personalised investment advice. Past index performance does not guarantee future returns. For grievances, visit SEBI's SCORES portal (scores.sebi.gov.in).
